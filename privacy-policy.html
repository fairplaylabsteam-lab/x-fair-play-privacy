🛡️ Privacy-First Commitment: We strictly respect your privacy. X Fair Play – AI Reply & Follow Manager does NOT operate external tracking databases, does NOT sell user data, and never accesses your passwords or private credentials. All actions are client-side and user-directed.

1. Introduction & Single Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how X Fair Play – AI Reply & Follow Manager ("Extension", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you install and use our Chrome browser extension.

The single core purpose of the Extension is to provide a productivity enhancement toolkit for users on X (formerly Twitter) to:

  • Manage Following & Follower Lists: Organize your network by identifying non-mutual followings, whitelisting VIP accounts, and safely executing user-directed batch follow/unfollow operations directly on the official X web interface.
  • Draft Contextual AI Replies: Provide an inline writing assistant directly below tweets on X, allowing users to generate natural, context-aware reply suggestions using their own personal AI API keys (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, xAI Grok, Groq, or OpenRouter).

2. Data Collection, Storage, and Processing Architecture

A. 100% Client-Side Follow Management

  • No Credentials Collected: The extension operates entirely within your existing active session on x.com. It never requests, stores, or transmits your account password, email, or authentication tokens.
  • Local Storage Only: All user configurations (custom whitelist handles, daily action limits, task delays, UI language, and action logs) are stored strictly locally on your machine using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API.

B. AI Reply Generation & Direct Provider API Calls

  • User-Provided API Keys: Users input their own API key for their chosen AI provider. These keys are stored strictly in chrome.storage.local on the user's computer. They are NEVER sent to the developer or any unauthorized server.
  • Direct HTTPS Communication: When you click an AI reply button, the text of that specific tweet and your selected persona instructions are sent directly and encrypted over HTTPS from your browser to your selected AI provider's official API endpoint (e.g. generativelanguage.googleapis.com or api.openai.com).
  • Zero Intermediary Logging: The extension has no developer backend or middleman proxy. The generated reply text is returned directly to your browser and placed into Twitter's composer for your review before you post.
  • No Data Retention or Training: The developer does not collect, log, store, or train models on your tweets, prompts, or generated replies.

C. No Analytics or Third-Party Tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, telemetry scripts, ad networks, tracking pixels, or third-party cookies.

3. Chrome Permissions Used & Justifications

In accordance with Chrome Web Store Developer Policies, the Extension requests only the minimum required permissions necessary to execute its declared core functionality:

Permission Purpose & Strict Justification
storage Required to store user settings, custom whitelist handles, delay preferences, UI language, and user-provided AI API keys locally on your device via chrome.storage.local.
sidePanel Allows the extension to open in Chrome's native Side Panel alongside your active X browsing tab for a seamless multitasking experience.
notifications Sends desktop notifications to alert you when a follow/unfollow batch action completes or when a rate-limit cooldown timer has finished.
tabs Enables the extension to detect if an active X (x.com) tab is already open and navigate to the relevant following/follower list when you initiate a scan.
alarms Schedules background countdown timers (such as the 40-minute rate-limit cooldown) via chrome.alarms, ensuring the timer continues accurately even if the extension popup is closed.
